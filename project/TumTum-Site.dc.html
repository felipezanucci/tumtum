COMO FUNCIONA

Você vive a noite.
A gente cuida do resto.

01 Conecta o relógio que você já usa

Sem aparelho novo, sem complicação. Seu smartwatch de sempre já registra tudo o que a gente precisa.

02 Vai pro evento e esquece da gente

03 Depois, a revelação

A TumTum cruza sua noite com a timeline do evento e te mostra qual momento foi o seu. Aí é só escolher o card e postar.

WHAT TUMTUM DOES

Everyone filmed the show.
Nobody recorded what they felt.

You keep the ticket stub, the wristband, the setlist screenshot. TumTum keeps the part that matters: the exact moment you lost your composure — with the time, the song and the proof.

HOW IT WORKS

You live the night.
We handle the rest.

01 Connect the watch you already own

No new gadget, no fuss. The smartwatch you already wear records everything we need.

02 Go to the event and forget about us

Sing, scream, suffer through the penalty. The watch stays on your wrist doing its job, like it always has.

03 Then, the reveal

TumTum crosses your night with the event timeline and shows you which moment was yours. Then you just pick a card and post it.
