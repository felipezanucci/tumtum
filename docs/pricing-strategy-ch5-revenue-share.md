# TumTum Pricing Strategy — Chapter 5: Artist/Athlete Revenue Share Model

---

## 5.1 The Strategic Importance of Artist/Athlete Participation

The artist/athlete HR comparison feature is TumTum's most powerful differentiator. No other platform offers "See how your heartbeat synced with your favorite artist during the show." This feature:

1. **Drives Pro subscriptions** — it's the #1 conversion trigger
2. **Creates viral content** — "82% in sync with Neymar" is inherently shareable
3. **Generates earned media** — when an artist shares their TumTum data, their millions of followers see the brand
4. **Builds a moat** — exclusive artist partnerships are hard for competitors to replicate

But this feature ONLY works if artists and athletes actually participate. The revenue share model must make participation a no-brainer for them.

---

## 5.2 Revenue Share Recommendation: 75/25 (Artist Gets 75%)

### The Split

| Party | Share | Rationale |
|-------|-------|-----------|
| **Artist/Athlete** | **75%** | High share encourages early adoption. The artist brings their audience (millions of followers). Their participation IS the product. |
| **TumTum** | **25%** | TumTum provides the platform, technology, card generation, and distribution. 25% is sustainable at scale because volume compensates for lower margin. |

### What Revenue Is Being Split?

The 75/25 split applies to **revenue directly attributable to the artist/athlete comparison feature:**

| Revenue Source | Split Applies? | Example |
|----------------|:-------------:|---------|
| Microtx: Artist comparison unlock (R$ 7,90/event) | Yes | Fan pays R$ 7,90 to compare with Anitta. Anitta gets R$ 5,93. TumTum gets R$ 1,97. |
| Pro subscription (portion attributed to artist feature) | Yes, proportionally | See attribution model below. |
| Sponsored comparison cards (brand pays for branded template) | Yes | Budweiser sponsors Anitta comparison cards. Revenue split 75/25. |
| B2B data insights about fan engagement | No | Aggregated/anonymized data sold to event promoters is 100% TumTum revenue. |
| Standard Pro features (non-artist cards, friend comparison) | No | This is TumTum's own product. No split needed. |

### Subscription Revenue Attribution to Artists

When a Pro subscriber uses the artist comparison feature, TumTum attributes a portion of their subscription to the artist pool:

```
Monthly Pro subscription: R$ 14,90

Attribution model:
├── If subscriber used artist comparison this month:
│   └── 30% of subscription (R$ 4,47) goes to the artist pool
│       └── Split proportionally among artists compared that month
│       └── Artist gets 75% of their share = R$ 3,35 per artist (if only 1)
│
├── If subscriber did NOT use artist comparison:
│   └── 0% goes to artist pool (100% TumTum revenue)
│
└── The 30% attribution rate is based on survey data showing
    artist comparison is the #1 reason ~30% of users subscribe.
```

**Example with real numbers:**

```
Scenario: Anitta performs at Allianz Parque, 40,000 attendees

Assumptions:
├── 5,000 TumTum users at the event (12.5% penetration)
├── 500 are Pro subscribers (10% of TumTum users)
│   └── All 500 use artist comparison (included in Pro)
│   └── Attribution: 500 x R$ 4,47 x 75% = R$ 1,676 to Anitta
│
├── 2,000 are free users
│   └── 15% buy microtx comparison (R$ 7,90 each)
│   └── 300 purchases x R$ 7,90 x 75% = R$ 1,777 to Anitta
│
├── 2,500 are free users who don't purchase
│   └── R$ 0 to Anitta (but they still see the feature, creating FOMO)
│
└── TOTAL to Anitta for ONE show: ~R$ 3,453
    TOTAL to TumTum for ONE show: ~R$ 1,551 (from comparison feature alone)
    + subscription revenue from non-comparison features
    + future microtx from converted free users
```

## 5.3 Why 75/25 (Not 50/50 or 80/20)

### Benchmark Comparison

| Platform | Creator Share | Platform Share | Context |
|----------|:------------:|:--------------:|---------|
| Cameo | 75% | 25% | Celebrity sets price. 1:1 video messages. |
| OnlyFans | 80% | 20% | Creator-driven. Platform is pure infrastructure. |
| YouTube | 55% | 45% | Massive audience. Platform provides distribution + ads. |
| Twitch | 50-70% | 30-50% | Live streaming. Platform provides real-time infrastructure. |
| Socios (Fan Tokens) | ~50% | ~50% | Crypto tokens. High platform complexity. |
| Patreon | 88-95% | 5-12% | Membership. Platform is payment infrastructure only. |
| **TumTum** | **75%** | **25%** | **Artist provides audience + biometric data. TumTum provides tech + distribution.** |

### Why Not 50/50?

- **50/50 is too aggressive for a new platform.** TumTum has no audience leverage yet. Artists are doing TumTum a favor by participating early. The 75% share says: "We respect that YOUR fanbase is what makes this work."
- At scale (1M+ users), TumTum could renegotiate toward 60/40, but starting generous builds loyalty and word-of-mouth among artist managers.

### Why Not 80/20 or 90/10?

- **TumTum needs margin to survive.** Card generation, infrastructure, payment processing (3-5% fees from payment gateways), and support all cost money.
- At 80/20, TumTum would earn R$ 1,58 per R$ 7,90 microtransaction. After payment processing (~R$ 0,40), that's R$ 1,18. Not sustainable.
- 75/25 yields R$ 1,97 per microtx, minus ~R$ 0,40 processing = R$ 1,57 net. Tight but viable at volume.

### Why 75/25 Is the Sweet Spot

```
Artist's perspective:
├── "I wear the device for 2 hours during my show" (minimal effort)
├── "I get 75% of all revenue my participation generates"
├── "My fans get a cool new way to engage with me"
├── "Free marketing — every comparison card has my name on it"
└── Decision: "Why wouldn't I do this?"

TumTum's perspective:
├── "We get 25% of comparison revenue" 
├── "The artist brings their audience to our platform"
├── "Artist posts about TumTum = millions in free impressions"
├── "Artist comparison drives Pro subscriptions (we keep 70% of non-comparison sub revenue)"
└── "The 25% margin is supplemented by massive user acquisition value"
```

## 5.4 Payment Structure by Participant Type

### Soccer Players

| Tier | Examples | Recommended Deal | Monthly Estimate |
|------|----------|-----------------|------------------|
| **Superstar (international fame)** | Neymar, Vinicius Jr., Endrick | Fixed advance R$ 20-50K/month + 75% rev share | R$ 30-80K total |
| **Brasileirao star** | Top scorers, fan favorites at big clubs | 75% rev share only (no advance) | R$ 5-20K (grows with TumTum user base) |
| **Rising talent** | Young players, Série A regulars | 75% rev share only | R$ 1-5K |
| **Emerging / Série B** | Up-and-coming players | 75% rev share + free brand exposure package | R$ 200-2K |

**Important:** Start with Brasileirao stars and rising talent. They're accessible, motivated by exposure, and their fan engagement drives meaningful revenue. Superstars come later when TumTum has proven the model.

### Soccer Clubs (Institutional Partnerships)

| Tier | Examples | Recommended Deal |
|------|----------|-----------------|
| **Big 12 clubs** | Flamengo, Corinthians, Palmeiras, São Paulo, Santos, etc. | Co-marketing deal: TumTum gets official club integration. Club gets R$ 2-5K/month minimum guarantee + 10% of all revenue from fans identified as club supporters. |
| **Mid-tier clubs** | Fortaleza, Bahia, Athletico-PR, etc. | Revenue share only: Club gets 10% of comparison revenue from their players + co-branding on cards. |
| **Smaller clubs / Série B** | Lower-table Série A, Série B | Free partnership: Club promotes TumTum to fans, TumTum provides analytics dashboard for fan engagement. |

**Note:** The club's 10% comes OUT OF TumTum's 25% share, not the player's 75%. So the effective split becomes: Player 75% / Club 10% / TumTum 15%. This is tight but worth it for the institutional access and credibility.

### Musicians / Artists

| Tier | Examples | Recommended Deal | Monthly Estimate |
|------|----------|-----------------|------------------|
| **Megastar** | Anitta, Ludmilla, Ivete Sangalo | Fixed advance R$ 10-30K/month + 75% rev share | R$ 20-60K total |
| **Major artist** | Sertanejo stars, popular MPB/funk artists | 75% rev share only | R$ 5-15K |
| **Rising artist** | Regional acts with 500K-2M followers | 75% rev share only | R$ 1-5K |
| **Emerging / indie** | Up-and-coming artists, local bands | 75% rev share + free promotion on TumTum's channels | R$ 100-1K |

**Key insight for musicians:** Artists who perform frequently (sertanejo artists doing 15-20 shows/month) generate more revenue than artists who do occasional mega-shows. The revenue share model rewards frequency, which naturally attracts the artists most valuable to TumTum.

## 5.6 Revenue Share Phase Evolution

As TumTum grows, the revenue share model evolves:

| Phase | TumTum Users | Artist Share | TumTum Share | Rationale |
|-------|-------------|:----------:|:----------:|-----------|
| **Phase 0 — Launch** | 0-50K | 75% | 25% | Must attract artists. TumTum has no leverage. Generosity builds relationships. |
| **Phase 1 — Growth** | 50K-500K | 75% | 25% | Maintain the rate. Artists are still the draw. Changing terms = broken trust. |
| **Phase 2 — Scale** | 500K-2M | 70% | 30% | TumTum now provides meaningful audience. New artist contracts at 70/30. Existing artists grandfathered at 75/25. |
| **Phase 3 — Dominance** | 2M+ | 65% | 35% | TumTum IS the platform. Artists want to be on it. New contracts at 65/35. Top-tier exclusives negotiated individually. |

**Golden rule:** NEVER retroactively change terms for existing partners. Grandfathering early partners at 75/25 forever is a loyalty investment that pays back in advocacy and retention.

## 5.7 Cost Structure for Artist/Athlete Feature

| Cost Component | Per-Event Cost | Notes |
|---------------|---------------|-------|
| Artist device setup & sync | ~R$ 0 (they use existing wearable) | Apple Watch or similar. TumTum provides setup support. |
| HR data ingestion & processing | R$ 0,02-0,05 per user comparison | Server compute for matching artist + fan HR curves. |
| Comparison card generation | R$ 0,05-0,10 per card | Pillow/ImageMagick generation on Celery workers. |
| CDN storage & serving | R$ 0,01-0,03 per card | Cloudflare R2 storage. Negligible at scale. |
| Payment processing (Pix/card) | 3-5% of transaction | Stripe or local processor. ~R$ 0,24-0,40 per R$ 7,90 microtx. |
| **Total cost per comparison card** | **~R$ 0,35-0,60** | |
| **Revenue per comparison (microtx)** | **R$ 7,90** | |
| **TumTum's 25% share** | **R$ 1,97** | |
| **TumTum net after costs** | **~R$ 1,37-1,62** | **~17-20% net margin on comparison feature** |

This is a healthy margin that improves at scale as infrastructure costs decrease per unit.
