## 2.1 Primary Outcomes Customers Pay For

Understanding what customers actually value — not what we think they value — is the foundation of pricing. For TumTum, customers pay for **emotional proof and social currency**, not data or technology.

### The Core Value Hierarchy

```
   Priority 1 (Must Have)         Priority 2 (Want)           Priority 3 (Delight)
   ─────────────────────         ─────────────────           ────────────────────
   "Show me MY moment"           "Make it beautiful"         "Compare me to my idol"
   
   → See HR curve synced         → Premium card templates    → Artist/athlete HR
     to event timeline           → HD export, no watermark     comparison
   → Identify peak moments       → Video/animated cards      → Sync percentage
   → Basic shareable card        → Multiple peaks per event  → Leaderboards
```

### Outcome #1: Emotional Validation — "I was THERE and I felt THAT"

**What it is:** Seeing your heart rate spike during the winning goal or the chorus of your favorite song. The proof that the moment was real and your body responded.

**Why they pay:** This is identity reinforcement. "I'm a real fan. Look how my heart reacted." In Brazilian culture, where passion for football and music is deeply tied to personal identity, this proof has immense emotional value.

**Segment differences:**

| Segment | Emotional Trigger | Example |
|---------|------------------|---------|
| Soccer | Goal by your team, penalty save, last-minute victory | "My heart hit 165 BPM when Palmeiras scored at 92'" |
| Concerts | Favorite song live, encore, artist interaction | "My heart peaked at 172 BPM during 'Evidencias' live" |

### Outcome #2: Social Currency — "Look what I experienced"

**What it is:** A visually stunning share card that communicates "I was at this event and this is how I felt" — optimized for Instagram Stories, TikTok, X, and WhatsApp.

**Why they pay:** Social media in Brazil is a primary form of self-expression. 150M+ social media users, among the most engaged globally. A TumTum card is a new format for showing "I was there" — better than a blurry concert photo.

**Willingness to pay drivers:**
- Card quality (premium templates vs. basic)
- Watermark removal (social status — "I paid for this")
- Video/animated cards (higher engagement on social platforms)
- Customization (choose which peaks to highlight)

### Outcome #3: Competitive Comparison — "How do I compare?"

**What it is:** Comparing your heart rate with friends who attended the same event, or (in Phase 1) with the artist/athlete themselves.

**Why they pay:** Brazilians are intensely competitive and social. Cartola FC (fantasy football) has 10M+ users driven by competition. The "sync percentage with your idol" feature taps into the same psychology but with real biometric data.

**This is the premium differentiator.** No other app offers this. It justifies the subscription alone.

## 2.2 Features: Table Stakes vs. Differentiators

### Table Stakes (Must Be Free)

These features MUST be free. Gating them would kill growth and feel exploitative given that users are sharing their health data.

| Feature | Why It Must Be Free |
|---------|-------------------|
| Connect wearable device | Setup friction must be zero. Paywalling device connection = DOA. |
| Attend events & record HR | The core data collection. Charging to record kills the funnel. |
| View your HR curve | Users contributed their data. Showing it back is the minimum. |
| See top 3 peaks per event | The "aha moment." This is what makes users say "wow" and want more. |
| Generate 1 basic share card per event | THE viral mechanism. Every free card = free marketing for TumTum. |
| View event timeline (setlist/match events) | Context for the HR curve. Without it, the data is meaningless. |

**Critical principle:** The free tier must deliver the complete "aha moment" — user sees their HR spike during the chorus, generates a card, shares it. If ANY step in this loop is paywalled, virality dies.

### True Differentiators (Premium Features)

These features create clear upgrade motivation because they enhance the experience AFTER the user already loves the free product.

| Feature | Differentiation Power | Why Users Pay |
|---------|----------------------|---------------|
| **Unlimited premium card templates** | HIGH | Basic card is good. Premium cards are stunning. Social media users care deeply about aesthetics. |
| **No watermark on cards** | HIGH | Watermark = "I use the free version." Removing it is a status signal. Same psychology as Canva Pro. |
| **HD & video/animated cards** | HIGH | Animated cards get 3-5x more engagement on Instagram/TikTok. Clear, measurable value. |
| **All peaks (unlimited)** | MEDIUM | Free shows top 3. Pro shows every peak. Power users want the full picture. |
| **Friend comparison** | MEDIUM-HIGH | Compare HR with friends at the same event. Social competition drives upgrades. |
| **Artist/athlete comparison** | VERY HIGH | The killer feature. "I was 78% in sync with Neymar." Exclusive, unique, shareable. |
| **Event history & collection** | MEDIUM | Free keeps last 5 events. Pro keeps unlimited history. Memory/nostalgia value. |
| **Peak highlights reel** | MEDIUM | Auto-generated video of your top moments across multiple events. Great for year-in-review. |
| **Priority card generation** | LOW-MEDIUM | Skip the queue during high-traffic events (post-match, post-concert). Convenience. |

## 2.3 Willingness to Pay Analysis — Brazilian Market

### Reference Price Anchors

Brazilian consumers evaluate TumTum's price against these existing subscriptions:

| App | Monthly Price (BRL) | Usage Pattern | Perceived Value |
|-----|-------------------|---------------|-----------------|
| Amazon Prime | R$ 19,90 | Daily (video + shipping) | Extremely high (bundle) |
| Spotify | R$ 21,90 | Daily (music) | High (essential) |
| YouTube Premium | R$ 24,90 | Daily (video) | Medium-high |
| Strava | R$ 31,99 | Weekly (fitness) | Medium (niche) |
| Globoplay | R$ 24,90 | Weekly (streaming) | Medium |
| **TumTum Pro** | **R$ 14,90** | **Periodic (events)** | **Must feel like a steal** |

### Why R$ 14,90 Is the Sweet Spot

1. **Below the "daily use" threshold.** Apps used daily (Spotify, Netflix) command R$ 20-30. TumTum is used around events (2-8x/month). Pricing at R$ 14,90 acknowledges this honestly, building trust.

2. **Above the "throwaway" zone.** Below R$ 9,90, the product feels cheap and low-value. R$ 14,90 signals quality while remaining accessible.

3. **Annual option creates urgency.** R$ 119,90/year = R$ 9,99/month effective rate. This crosses the psychological R$ 10 barrier and aligns with "impulse buy" territory.

4. **Minimum wage math.** R$ 14,90 = ~1% of minimum wage (R$ 1.518). Research shows Brazilian Classes B/C will spend up to 1.5% of monthly income on a single entertainment subscription they value.

### Willingness to Pay by Segment

#### Soccer Fans

| User Profile | Events/Month | WTP (Monthly) | Likely Behavior |
|-------------|-------------|---------------|-----------------|
| Casual fan (watches on TV, goes to 1-2 matches/year) | 0-1 | R$ 0 (free tier) | Uses free tier for occasional matches. May buy 1 premium card for a derby. |
| Regular matchgoer (season ticket, 2-4 matches/month) | 2-4 | R$ 9,90-14,90 | Strong subscription candidate. Multiple events/month justify recurring cost. |
| Ultra/Fanatic (every match, travels for away games) | 4-8 | R$ 14,90-19,90 | Guaranteed subscriber. Will want every card, comparison features, full history. |

**Key soccer insight:** The Brasileirao season (April-December) creates a natural 9-month subscription window. Outside season, churn risk is high. Annual pricing mitigates this.

#### Concert/Festival Fans

| User Profile | Events/Month | WTP (Monthly) | Likely Behavior |
|-------------|-------------|---------------|-----------------|
| Occasional concertgoer (2-3 shows/year) | 0-1 | R$ 0 (free or microtx) | Uses free tier or buys R$ 4,90 premium card per event. |
| Active music fan (1-2 shows/month) | 1-2 | R$ 9,90-14,90 | Good subscription candidate, especially during festival season (Sep-Dec). |
| Festival enthusiast (Lollapalooza, Rock in Rio, multiple shows) | 2-5 | R$ 14,90 | Subscriber during peak season. Multiple events per festival = high card generation. |

**Key concert insight:** Concerts are emotional and unique — "I'll never see this show again." This FOMO drives higher per-card WTP (R$ 4,90 feels cheap for a once-in-a-lifetime memory). Microtransactions may outperform subscriptions in this segment.

### Price Sensitivity by Socioeconomic Class

| Class | % of Brazilian Population | Monthly Digital Budget | TumTum Opportunity |
|-------|--------------------------|----------------------|-------------------|
| A (High income) | ~5% | R$ 100-200+ | Will subscribe without thinking. Target for annual plan. |
| B (Upper middle) | ~20% | R$ 50-100 | Primary subscription target. R$ 14,90 is easy to justify. |
| C (Middle) | ~35% | R$ 20-50 | Price-sensitive. Annual plan (R$ 9,99/mo) or microtransactions. |
| D/E (Lower) | ~40% | R$ 0-20 | Free tier only. Still valuable for virality (they share cards too). |

**Total addressable market for paid conversion: Classes A+B+C = ~60% of population, but realistically 5-10% conversion of active users.**

## 2.4 Conversion Triggers — What Makes Free Users Upgrade

Based on patterns from Strava, Spotify, and Canva in the Brazilian market:

### Trigger 1: The "Ugly Watermark" Moment
User creates an amazing card showing their 168 BPM peak during the Corinthians derby goal. They go to share on Instagram Stories... and see the TumTum watermark. "R$ 4,90 to remove it? Done."

### Trigger 2: The "I Want the Animated One" Moment
User sees a friend's animated TumTum card on TikTok with the HR curve pulsing to the beat. Their own card is a static PNG. "How do I get THAT version?"

### Trigger 3: The "Compare With My Friend" Moment
Two friends attended the same Lollapalooza set. Both have TumTum. Free tier shows individual curves. Pro shows side-by-side comparison with sync percentage. Competitive instinct kicks in.

### Trigger 4: The "Season Is Starting" Moment
Brasileirao kicks off in April. The matchgoer knows they'll attend 15+ matches. R$ 14,90/month for unlimited premium cards vs. R$ 4,90 x 15 = R$ 73,50. Subscription is the obvious choice.

### Trigger 5: The "Artist Comparison" Moment (Phase 1)
Anitta shares her heart rate from a show. Fans at the same show can see "You were 82% in sync with Anitta." This is the nuclear conversion trigger — exclusive, emotional, shareable, and only available to Pro users.
