# TumTum Pricing Strategy — Chapter 3: Tier Structure, Naming & Price Points

---

## 3.1 Number of Tiers: Two (Free + Pro)

### Rationale for Two Tiers (Not Three or Four)

| Factor | Decision | Why |
|--------|----------|-----|
| Phase 0 MVP | 2 tiers max | Not enough user data to justify segmentation into 3+ tiers. Premature tier splitting wastes engineering time and confuses users. |
| Brazilian consumer behavior | Fewer choices = faster decisions | Brazilian app users show higher conversion rates with binary choices ("Free or Pro?") vs. multi-tier comparisons. Paradox of choice is real. |
| TumTum usage pattern | Not complex enough for 3 tiers | Unlike B2B SaaS (where teams have different needs), TumTum's value is consistent: see HR, make cards, share. More/better of the same thing, not fundamentally different use cases. |
| Operational simplicity | Less support, less confusion | Two tiers = one upgrade path. No "which plan is right for me?" friction. |
| Future flexibility | Can split later with data | Once TumTum has 50K+ users and real usage data, we can identify if a third tier (e.g., "Tumtum Ultra" with artist comparisons) makes sense. Adding a tier is easy; removing one is painful. |

### When to Add a Third Tier

Add a third tier ONLY when:
- Artist/athlete comparison feature launches (Phase 1) AND demand data confirms it warrants a separate price point
- B2B/corporate use case emerges (e.g., event promoters wanting analytics dashboards)
- User research shows a clear segment willing to pay 2x the Pro price for specific features

## 3.2 Tier Positioning & Target Customers

### Tier 1: TumTum Free

**Target customer:** Everyone. Every person at every event. This is the top of the funnel.

**Positioning:** "Your heart was there. See it. Share it."

**Psychology:** The free tier is NOT a trial. It's a complete product that delivers the core "aha moment." Users should feel grateful, not frustrated. Frustration = uninstall. Gratitude = eventual upgrade.

**Target personas:**
- First-time user exploring the app
- Casual fan who goes to 2-3 events per year
- Class C/D users who can't justify any subscription
- Anyone in the "awareness" phase of the funnel

### Tier 2: TumTum Pro

**Target customer:** Regular event-goers who want premium social content and competitive features.

**Positioning:** "Every beat. Every moment. Beautifully yours."

**Psychology:** Pro users are paying for two things: (1) better-looking content for social media, and (2) competitive/social features (friend comparison, full peak history). It's about status and completeness.

**Target personas:**
- Soccer season ticket holders (2-4 matches/month)
- Active concertgoers (1-2+ events/month)
- Social media-conscious users (Instagram/TikTok active)
- Friend groups who attend events together (comparison feature)
- Dedicated fans who want their complete emotional history

## 3.3 Naming Strategy

### "TumTum" + "TumTum Pro"

| Name | Rationale |
|------|-----------|
| **TumTum** (free) | The product IS TumTum. No qualifier needed. Using "Free" or "Basic" makes the product feel lesser. Just "TumTum." |
| **TumTum Pro** | "Pro" is universally understood in Brazil (Spotify, Canva, LinkedIn all use "Pro" or "Premium" in the Brazilian market). It signals "better" without being pretentious. Short, clean, memorable. |

### Names Rejected

| Name | Why Rejected |
|------|-------------|
| TumTum Premium | Sounds like insurance or banking. Too corporate for an emotional product. |
| TumTum Plus | Overused (Disney+, Apple TV+, ESPN+). Doesn't convey enough upgrade value. |
| TumTum VIP | Too nightclub/events-industry. Could feel cheesy. |
| TumTum Gold/Black | Doesn't connect to the product's emotional core. Arbitrary. |
| TumTum Pulse | Cool but confusing — is it a different product? A feature? |

### Why "Pro" Wins

- **Familiar:** Every Brazilian knows what "Pro" means on an app.
- **Aspirational without being exclusive:** "Pro" says "you're serious about this" without saying "you're rich."
- **Short:** Fits in UI badges, push notifications, and social proof ("Maria uses TumTum Pro").
- **Extensible:** If a third tier is needed later, "TumTum Ultra" or "TumTum Max" naturally sits above "Pro."

## 3.4 Price Points & Rationale

### TumTum Pro Pricing

| Billing Cycle | Price | Effective Monthly | Discount | Rationale |
|--------------|-------|-------------------|----------|-----------|
| **Monthly** | R$ 14,90/month | R$ 14,90 | — | Anchor price. Below Spotify (R$ 21,90) and YouTube Premium (R$ 24,90). Honest pricing for a periodic-use app. |
| **Annual** | R$ 119,90/year | R$ 9,99/month | 33% off | Crosses the R$ 10 psychological barrier. Creates "no-brainer" feeling. Locks in users through low-activity periods. |
| **Seasonal** (soccer) | R$ 79,90/season (~8 months) | R$ 9,99/month | 33% off | Matches Brasileirao calendar (April-December). Perfect for soccer-only users. Reduces churn from "I'm paying but no matches." |

### Microtransaction Pricing (For Non-Subscribers)

| Item | Price | Rationale |
|------|-------|-----------|
| Premium card (single, no watermark, premium template) | R$ 4,90 | Impulse-buy zone. Same range as FutebolCard digital collectibles. User just had an emotional experience — willingness to pay is highest immediately post-event. |
| Animated/video card (single) | R$ 6,90 | Higher production value = higher price. Still below R$ 10 impulse threshold. |
| Card bundle (3 premium cards) | R$ 9,90 | Discount for buying ahead. Good for festival-goers who know they'll want multiple cards. |
| Artist/athlete comparison unlock (single event) | R$ 7,90 | Premium microtransaction for the killer feature. Higher WTP because it's exclusive and highly shareable. |

### Price Point Validation

**Comparison with Brazilian market:**

```
R$ 4,90 per card vs. alternatives:
├── Coffee at Starbucks BR:     R$ 15-25
├── FutebolCard digital pack:   R$ 4,90-19,90  
├── Uber ride (short, SP):      R$ 12-18
├── Movie ticket (off-peak):    R$ 20-30
├── Concert photo print:        R$ 8-15
└── Instagram "boost" a post:   R$ 6+ minimum
    
Conclusion: R$ 4,90 for a premium memory card feels cheap
            compared to what Brazilians spend on entertainment.
```

**Subscription comparison:**

```
R$ 14,90/month vs. competitor subscriptions:
├── Spotify:           R$ 21,90  (daily use)    → TumTum is 32% cheaper
├── YouTube Premium:   R$ 24,90  (daily use)    → TumTum is 40% cheaper
├── Strava:            R$ 31,99  (weekly use)   → TumTum is 53% cheaper
├── Amazon Prime:      R$ 19,90  (daily use)    → TumTum is 25% cheaper
├── Globoplay:         R$ 24,90  (weekly use)   → TumTum is 40% cheaper
└── Apple Fitness+:    R$ 21,90  (weekly use)   → TumTum is 32% cheaper

Conclusion: R$ 14,90 positions TumTum as the cheapest premium 
            subscription in a user's stack. Easy to justify keeping.
```

## 3.5 Pricing Page UX Principles

For the Brazilian market, the pricing page must:

1. **Show monthly and annual side-by-side** with the annual savings highlighted ("Economize 33%" in green).
2. **Default-select the annual plan.** Annual subscribers have 3x lower churn.
3. **Show the effective monthly price** for annual (R$ 9,99/mo) — not just the total R$ 119,90.
4. **Accept Pix and boleto.** ~30% of Brazilian digital payments are via Pix. Not supporting it = losing 30% of potential subscribers.
5. **Offer credit card installments** (parcelamento). R$ 119,90 in 12x R$ 9,99 is psychologically easier than a single charge.
6. **No comparison table with 10+ features.** Show 3-4 key differences max. The decision should take < 10 seconds.
7. **Social proof on the pricing page.** "12.000 fãs já usam TumTum Pro" with real numbers once available.

## 3.6 Seasonal Soccer Pass — Deep Dive

The seasonal pass is a strategic pricing option specifically for soccer fans:

**Brasileirao Season Pass: R$ 79,90 (~April to December)**

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Duration | ~8 months (matches Brasileirao calendar) |
| Effective monthly rate | R$ 9,99/month |
| Target user | Soccer fan who attends matches regularly but doesn't go to concerts |
| Advantage over monthly | 33% savings + no decision fatigue each month |
| Advantage over annual | Doesn't pay for Jan-March when there are no matches |
| Renewal strategy | Auto-renew each season with 15-day advance notice |

**Why this matters:** Brazilian soccer is intensely seasonal. Fans are highly engaged April-December and practically dormant January-March (except for pre-season). A season pass respects this reality and prevents the "why am I paying when there are no games?" churn that would kill monthly subscriptions in the off-season.
