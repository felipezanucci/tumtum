# TumTum Values

The principles that guide everything we build, every decision we make, and every beat we capture.

---

## 1. Fortune Favors the BOLD

We don't play it safe. TumTum was born from the belief that technology should capture what makes us feel alive — and building that requires courage.

We value people who take risks, who dare, who think differently. The safe path is the path already taken by everyone else. We choose the uncharted one.

**What this means in practice:**

- **Propose the crazy idea.** The best features start as "that's impossible." Say it anyway.
- **Ship before it's perfect.** A bold experiment in production teaches more than a perfect plan in a doc.
- **Challenge the status quo.** "We've always done it this way" is not a reason. Question everything.
- **Own your bets.** Bold doesn't mean reckless. Take calculated risks, own the outcomes — wins and losses.
- **Speak up.** If something feels wrong, say it. If you see a better way, share it. Silence is the opposite of bold.

> *"The people who are crazy enough to think they can change the world are the ones who do."*

We're building something that has never existed before — a platform that turns heartbeats into stories. That takes audacity. That takes people who look at the impossible and say: *"Let's try it."*

Fortune favors the bold. Always has. Always will.

---

## 2. Better to Ask for Forgiveness Than Permission

We value people who take action. Waiting for the perfect green light means standing still while the world moves. At TumTum, bias for action beats analysis paralysis — every time.

This doesn't mean being careless. It means trusting your judgment, making the call, and moving forward. The worst thing you can do here is nothing.

**What this means in practice:**

- **Act first, refine later.** See a problem? Fix it. Don't wait for a meeting to discuss the meeting about the problem.
- **Trust yourself.** You were hired because you're good. You don't need five approvals to do what you know is right.
- **Move fast, learn faster.** A wrong decision made quickly teaches you more than the right decision made too late.
- **Unblock yourself.** Don't sit waiting on someone else. Find another way. Build a bridge. Kick the door down.
- **Take ownership.** Acting without permission means owning the result. Celebrate the wins, learn from the misses.

> *"In any moment of decision, the best thing you can do is the right thing. The worst thing you can do is nothing."*

We're a startup. Speed is oxygen. The person who sees the opportunity and grabs it will always beat the one who writes a proposal about grabbing it.

Take the shot. We'll figure out the rest together.
