# TumTum Values

The principles that guide everything we build, every decision we make, and every beat we capture.

---

## 1. Fortune Favors the BOLD

We don't play it safe. TumTum was born from the belief that technology should capture what makes us feel alive — and building that requires courage.

We value people who take risks, who dare, who think differently. The safe path is the path already taken by everyone else. We choose the uncharted one.

**What this means in practice:**

- **Propose the crazy idea.** The best features start as "that's impossible." Say it anyway.
- **Ship before it's perfect.** A bold experiment in production teaches more than a perfect plan in a doc.
- **Challenge the status quo.** "We've always done it this way" is not a reason. Question everything.
- **Own your bets.** Bold doesn't mean reckless. Take calculated risks, own the outcomes — wins and losses.
- **Speak up.** If something feels wrong, say it. If you see a better way, share it. Silence is the opposite of bold.

> *"The people who are crazy enough to think they can change the world are the ones who do."*

We're building something that has never existed before — a platform that turns heartbeats into stories. That takes audacity. That takes people who look at the impossible and say: *"Let's try it."*

Fortune favors the bold. Always has. Always will.
